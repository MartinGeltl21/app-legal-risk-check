# Legal sources and interpretation

Reference review date: **2026-10-06**. Verify applicable current primary sources before giving legal conclusions. This reference is not continuous legal monitoring. A social-media checklist inspired the topic selection; it is not authority. Do not conflate maximum penalties, statutory damages, actual damages, and their calculation units.

## COPPA

- [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
- [FTC children's privacy materials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/childrens-privacy)
- [FTC February 2026 age-verification policy statement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/02/ftc-issues-coppa-policy-statement-incentivize-use-age-verification-technologies-protect-children)

Distinguish child-directed, mixed-audience, and general-audience services, including actual knowledge of collection from children under 13. The rule was amended in 2025; the conditional 2026 enforcement statement may also matter. An enforcement statement is not repeal. Do not infer a universal age-field requirement or fixed penalty per child.

## Fonts and GDPR

- [Munich Regional Court I, 20 January 2022, 3 O 17493/20](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2022-N-612)
- [Hessian data protection authority on Google Fonts demands](https://datenschutz.hessen.de/datenschutz/internet-und-medien/google-fonts-abmahnungen)

The Munich decision awarded EUR 100 on its facts, not an automatic tariff per visitor. Remote browser requests can disclose personal data; locally served fonts avoid that particular transfer. This is not a universal conclusion about all external resources.

- [California Penal Code §631](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=631.)
- [California Penal Code §632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632.)
- [California Penal Code §637.2](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=637.2.)
- [Official 2016 amendment, AB 1671](https://leginfo.ca.gov/pub/15-16/bill/asm/ab_1651-1700/ab_1671_bill_20160930_chaptered.pdf)

Direct requests to the current §631 and §637.2 pages returned HTTP 403 during preparation; §632 is an additional source to consult. The historical amendment states USD 5,000 per violation or three times actual damages. It does not establish the current law by itself. Applicability to replay depends on facts, parties, data flows, consent, and case law. Do not substitute “session” for “violation.”

## Marketing and CAN-SPAM

- [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)

Primary purpose affects classification. Review sender/subject accuracy, advertising identification, postal address, and opt-out. The guide describes 30-day mechanism availability and 10-business-day implementation. Penalty limits may change. CAN-SPAM alone does not establish worldwide permission to send marketing.

## California automatic renewal

- [California Attorney General's automatic renewal guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/node/608083)
- [Business and Professions Code §17602](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=17602.)
- [Business and Professions Code §17603](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=17603.)

The Attorney General describes changes effective 1 July 2025, including express consent and easy cancellation. Check disclosures, confirmations, records, and notices against the current statute and contract date. Direct statute requests returned HTTP 403 during preparation. Do not broadly apply the “unconditional gift” claim to every digital service or checkout omission; verify the specific legal consequences.

## User content and DMCA

- [Copyright Office: Section 512](https://www.copyright.gov/512/)
- [Designated Agent Directory and registration entry point](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/)
- [Copyright Office agent FAQ and linked tutorials](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html)
- [17 USC Chapter 5, including §§504 and 512](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html)

At review, the FAQ lists USD 6 for a designation, amendment, or resubmission and renewal at least every three years. Verify before filing. Prepare the operator's legal name/address, alternate names, and agent contacts. Registered information becomes public; separate legal entities are not simply alternate names.

Safe harbor depends on statutory conditions beyond registration. Section 504 distinguishes actual and statutory damages; USD 150,000 can be an upper limit for willful infringement per work under statutory conditions, not a standard per-upload charge.
